05The route to a real job

Every project is a month-long interview.

Firms don't hire Terna members off a CV. They hire after watching you scope-check, build, miss, fix, and ship. By the time an offer comes, the risk question is already answered — for both of you. And when you're hired, the studio backfills your clients: your conversion is the model working, not a loose end.
